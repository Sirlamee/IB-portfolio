  • Cleanstate

    This animated video was created for CleanState to communicate its mission, services, and impact in a clear and engaging way. The goal was to simplify the brand’s message and present its value through compelling visual storytelling. Using motion design, the video translates CleanState’s objectives into an accessible narrative that educates viewers, builds trust, and strengthens brand awareness across digital platforms.

  • My Role

    Illustrator

    Motion Designer

  • Tools Used

    Adobe Illustrator

    Adobe After Effects

    Adobe Media Encoder

  • Goal

    The goal of this project was to clearly communicate CleanState’s mission and offerings while increasing awareness of its impact. The video aimed to educate viewers, reinforce brand credibility.

  • Design Approach

    The design approach centered on clarity, consistency, and purpose-driven storytelling. CleanState’s message was translated into simple visuals and smooth animations that align with the brand’s identity. A clean visual style, thoughtful pacing, and clear transitions were used to ensure the information was easy to follow, engaging, and impactful while maintaining a professional tone that reflects CleanState’s mission and values.

  • End Result

    The final animated video effectively communicated CleanState’s mission and value proposition, strengthening brand awareness and message clarity. It served as a versatile asset across digital channels, supporting outreach and engagement efforts
