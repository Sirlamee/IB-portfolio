  • MinzuBox Pre - Launch Video

    Ahead of Minzubox’s launch, we created a pre-launch motion video to clearly communicate the product’s value and build early momentum. As a new platform, Minzubox needed a simple, engaging way to introduce its core idea, spark curiosity, and position itself credibly in the market.

  • My Role

    Illustrator

    Motion Designer

  • Tools Used

    Adobe Illustrator

    Adobe After Effects

    Adobe Media Encoder

    Figma

  • Goal

    The aim of the motion video is to allow us explain the concept quickly, highlight key benefits, and create anticipation before release. It also served as a versatile asset for social media, landing pages, and announcements ensuring consistent messaging and stronger brand recall ahead of launch.

  • Storyboard

    The storyboard for the Minzubox pre-launch video was crafted to build curiosity and anticipation ahead of the product release. It focused on introducing the idea behind Minzubox without revealing too much, using short, impactful scenes to hint at key benefits and possibilities.

  • Design Approach

    The aim of the motion video is to allow us explain the concept quickly, highlight key benefits, and create anticipation before release. It also served as a versatile asset for social media, landing pages, and announcements ensuring consistent messaging and stronger brand recall ahead of launch.

  • End Result

    The pre-launch motion video successfully generated awareness and interest ahead of Minzubox’s release. It helped clarify the product offering, strengthened brand positioning, and increased engagement across pre-launch touchpoints. Success was measured through key indicators such as increased social media engagement, higher landing page visits, early sign-ups, and positive audience feedback—validating the video’s effectiveness as a launch marketing asset.
