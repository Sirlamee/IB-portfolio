  • Introduction To Web3

    This animated explainer video was created for the Web3 Lagos State community to educate and raise awareness about the evolution of the internet from Web1 to Web3. The goal was to simplify complex concepts and present Web3 in a clear, engaging, and accessible way for a broad audience. Through motion design and storytelling, the video bridges knowledge gaps, helps viewers understand the relevance of Web3, and supports the community’s mission of promoting Web3 education.

  • My Role

    Illustrator

    Motion Designer

  • Tools Used

    Adobe Illustrator

    Adobe After Effects

    Adobe Media Encoder

  • Goal

    The goal of this project was to educate and inform the Web3 Lagos State community by breaking down the evolution of the internet in a simple and engaging way.

  • Design Approach

    The design approach focused on clarity, simplicity, and storytelling. Complex Web3 concepts were translated into easy to understand visuals using clean illustrations, smooth motion, and a logical narrative flow. The animation style was intentionally minimal and engaging to maintain attention while guiding viewers through each stage of the internet’s evolution.

  • End Result

    The final video successfully delivered a clear and engaging introduction to Web3, making the evolution of the internet easier to understand for a wide audience. It supported the Web3 Lagos State community’s educational efforts and sparked meaningful conversations around Web3 concepts
