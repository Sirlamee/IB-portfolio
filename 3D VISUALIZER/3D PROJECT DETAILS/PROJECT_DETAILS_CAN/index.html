  • Treeze Visualizer

    This project involved creating a 3D product visualization and animation for Treeze, a canned beverage, with a focus on realistic modeling and dynamic presentation. I handled both the modeling and animation in Blender, ensuring accurate proportions, material details, and surface reflections.

  • Tools Used

    Blender

    Adobe After Effects

  • Goal

    The goal of this project was to create a visually engaging 3D representation of the Treeze beverage can that accurately conveys its branding, design, and premium appeal. The focus was on producing a digital visualization that could be used for marketing, social media, and promotional content, enhancing the product’s visibility and attractiveness.

  • Creative Direction

    The creative direction emphasized realism and vibrancy, using precise lighting, reflective materials, and dynamic camera angles to highlight the can’s shape, label, and texture. Smooth animation was incorporated to add energy and movement, creating a lively, modern, and brand-aligned visual experience.

  • End Result

    The final result is a dynamic 3D visualization and animation that presents the Treeze beverage can with realism and visual appeal. Detailed modeling, accurate materials, and fluid motion highlight the product’s design, branding, and surface quality.
