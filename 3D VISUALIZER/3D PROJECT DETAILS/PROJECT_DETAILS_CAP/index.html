  • Mercenary Trucker Hat Visualizer

    This project involved creating a high-quality 3D product visualization and animation for Mercenary, focusing on their signature Trucker Hat. I handled both the modeling and animation, aiming to capture the hat’s structure, textures, and branding details with precision. The visualization highlights the product’s craftsmanship and design, presenting it in a clean, stylish, and visually engaging way that aligns with Mercenary’s contemporary fashion identity.

  • Tools Used

    Blender

    Adobe After Effects

  • Goal

    The goal was to create a motion video which will help explain what minzubox is, how minzubox operates and also the benefit of minzubox. The aim is to ensure the video is a simple as possible and it breaks down every important facts about minzubox

  • Creative Direction

    The creative direction emphasized realism and elegance, with careful attention to lighting, material textures, and smooth animation to highlight the product’s design and packaging.The creative direction centered on a minimal yet bold aesthetic, using controlled lighting and subtle shadows to highlight the hat’s shape and materials. Smooth animation and dynamic camera angles were employed to give the product a sense of depth and presence.

  • End Result

    The final result is a high-quality 3D visualization and animation that presents the Mercenary Trucker Hat with precision and style. Realistic modeling, detailed textures, and dynamic camera movement bring the product to life, highlighting its craftsmanship and contemporary design.
