  • VSP Botanics product visualizer

    This project involved creating a high-quality 3D product visualization and animation for VSP Cosmetics, bringing the brand’s product to life in a digital space. I handled both the modeling and animation, focusing on realistic textures, precise detailing, and smooth motion to showcase the product’s premium quality

  • Tools Used

    Blender

    Adobe After Effects

  • Goal

    The goal of this project was to create a visually compelling 3D representation of VSP botanics that accurately reflects the brand’s premium quality and attention to detail. The focus was on producing content that could be used across digital platforms to enhance product visibility and engage consumers.

  • Creative Direction

    The creative direction emphasized realism and elegance, with careful attention to lighting, material textures, and smooth animation to highlight the product’s design and packaging.

  • End Result

    The final result is a refined 3D visualization and animation that presents VSP botanics in a premium and engaging way. Realistic modeling, detailed textures, and smooth motion highlight the product’s design and packaging, creating visuals that are both visually appealing and brand-aligned.
