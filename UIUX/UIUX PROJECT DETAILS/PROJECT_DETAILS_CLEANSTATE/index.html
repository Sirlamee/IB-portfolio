  • Project Overview

    Cleanstate is a premium cleaning service brand built around a simple belief that a clean space creates a better life.

  • Project Duration

    May 2024 - June 2024

  • My Role

    UI/UX Designer

    Motion Designer

  • Tools used

    Figma, Adobe illustrator, Adobe after effect.

  • Problem statement

    Cleaning services often feel generic, cluttered, and transactional. Cleanstate needed a digital identity that communicated trust, luxury, and simplicity while making it easy for users to book services with zero friction.

  • Objective / goal

    • Design an interface that feels:
    • Clean, calm, and premium
    • Easy to navigate for all users
    • Transparent in pricing and service options
    • Trustworthy and professional
    The experience needed to visually express the brand’s message:
    “A clean space brings peace of mind.”

  • Design Process

    Research:
    I explored competitor platforms and identified frustration points: overcrowded layouts, confusing booking flows, and lack of clarity in service types. This informed a direction focused on minimalism and clarity.

    Key Insights
    • Most cleaning service websites overload users with text, long forms, and unclear service categories. People prefer a quick, clean, and effortless way to book..
    • Users expect to book in 2–3 steps max. Long flows increase drop-off rates, especially for first-time customers..

  • Design Process

    I created simple, structured wireframes prioritizing:
    • Quick booking
    • Simple service categories
    • Calm visual hierarchy
    • Clear CTAs and clean spacing

  • High Fidelity/ Final Designs

    With the wireframes validated, I transitioned into high-fidelity design bringing Ziks to life with visual clarity, strong hierarchy.

    The final UI embraces a soft, premium aesthetic with:
    • Spacious layouts
    • Neutral tones
    • Gentle shadows
    • Clear typography
    • Polished service cards

    The interface feels refreshing and organized mirroring the service Cleanstate provides.

  • High Fidelity/ Final Designs

    Key Screens Delivered
    • Home / Hero Section
    • Service Categories
    • Booking Flow
    • Pricing Overview
    • About & Brand Promise
    • Contact / Scheduling

    Each screen maintains the brand’s minimal and luxurious tone

  • Final Designs

    The aim was to ensure the designs was intentionally stripped of noise. I used:
    • Soft neutrals
    • Ample white space
    • Subtle shadows
    • Clean typography

  • Project Outcome

    • 32 Early adopters signed up for access within the first week of announcement.
    • 70% of testers successfully created a working mini app without onboarding assistance.
    • High usability score from early test users evaluating clarity and simplicity.
