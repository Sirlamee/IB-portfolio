  • Project Overview

    Hollamedia is a professional Nigerian photographer, filmmaker, and creative director whose work spans portraits, events, fashion, lifestyle, and cinematic visuals. The goal of this project was to design a portfolio website that doesn’t just show photos but tells stories.

  • Project Duration

    March 2025 - June 2025

  • My Role

    UI/UX Designer

  • Tools used

    Figma

  • Objective / goal

    Design a portfolio experience that:
    • Showcases photography in a cinematic, immersive, and playful way
    • Makes the navigation feel like a smooth visual journey
    • Highlights his multi-disciplinary creativity (photo, film, creative direction)
    • Encourages clients to book shoots effortlessly
    • Works beautifully across devices

  • Design Research/Inspiration

    I explored global photographer websites, digital galleries, and creative director portfolios.
    Key insights:
    • Users are attracted to large visuals, smooth transitions, and minimal distractions
    • Personality builds trust
    • Motion brings life to artistic content
    I also analyzed Hollamedia’s visual style which includes warm tones, bold framing, expressive subjects and i designed around that energy.

  • Design Process

    Wireframe:
    Instead of traditional blocky wireframes, I approached this phase as designing the flow of a story, not just the structure of pages.
    I mapped:
    • How a user emotionally enters the site
    • Where the first “wow moment” should be
    • How the portfolio should feel like a gallery walkthrough
    • How film work should feel cinematic, not static
    • Where the call-to-action appears naturally without breaking immersion

  • High Fidelity/ Final Designs

    The final UI is bold, cinematic, and expressive.
    Visual decisions:
    • Playful typography that feels modern and artistic
    • Soft micro-interactions that guide the eye naturally
    • A neutral color palette so images remain the star
    • Magazine-style layouts for depth and personality
    • Film-inspired transitions for storytelling
    Hollamedia’s creativity is loud, so the design is intentionally quiet, letting his work speak.

  • Key Screens Delivered

    • Photography Portfolio
    A gallery that adapts to each category, portraits, weddings, lifestyle, events, editorial.
    • Film Showcase
    A video first layout with subtle overlays resembling film strips and frames.
    • About the Creator
    A storytelling-driven layout showcasing his journey, achievements, and artistic philosophy.
    • Archived Page
    A dedicated space for older works, experimental visuals, and retired projects organized in a way that feels like stepping into Hollamedia’s creative vault.
    • Behind-the-Scenes Page
    A documentary-style section showing BTS photos, set environments, lighting setups, camera placements, and candid moments from shoots.

  • Final Experience

    The final design feels:
    • Artistic but structured
    • Playful but professional
    • Cinematic but minimal
    • Personal but universal
