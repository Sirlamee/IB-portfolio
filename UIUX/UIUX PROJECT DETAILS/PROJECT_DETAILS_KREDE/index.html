  • Project Overview

    Krede Placement Agency is a premium childcare service platform created to give modern parents peace of mind, connecting them with certified, professionally trained nannies who support children’s development and family well-being.

  • Project Duration

    Dec 2024 - Jan 2025

  • My Role

    UI/UX Designer

  • Tools used

    Figma

  • Problem statement

    Parents often struggle to find reliable childcare providers who are both qualified and trustworthy. Existing platforms are either too generic, too crowded, or lack transparency about nanny training and standards.

  • Objective / goal

    Create an interface that feels:
    • Safe and reassuring
    • Simple and quick to navigate
    • Transparently professional
    • Warm and human-centered
    The core objective was to help parents quickly understand the value of certified, expertly trained nannies and feel confident making a booking.

  • Design Process

    Research:
    I explored the childcare service landscape and identified key parental concerns:
    • Safety and credibility of caregivers
    • Clear background and certification information
    • Easy booking and communication
    This informed a direction centered around trust, clarity, and emotional reassurance.

  • Design Process

    Wireframe:
    Before moving into visuals, I focused on creating wireframes that translated the emotional needs of parents into a clear and functional product experience
    I identified the most crucial questions parents try to answer within seconds:
    • Who will care for my child?
    • Are they trained and verified?
    • What safety measures are in place?
    • How can I monitor or stay updated?
    The first step was structuring the wireframes to surface these answers without users having to search.

  • High Fidelity/ Final Designs

    The final design uses a warm, soft visual language that reflects the heart of childcare.
    Key design choices:
    • Soft neutrals and gentle gradients for a peaceful atmosphere
    • Friendly typography that balances professionalism with approachability
    • Spacious layouts to reduce cognitive load
    • Clear nanny cards with training badges, certifications, and experience
    • Trust elements like testimonials and safety guidelines placed strategically
    The result is an interface that feels safe, premium, and parent-centered.

  • High Fidelity/ Final Designs

    Key Screens Delivered
    • Home / Hero Section
    • Service Categories
    • Booking Flow
    • Pricing Overview
    • About & Brand Promise
    • Contact / Scheduling

    Each screen maintains the brand’s minimal and luxurious tone

  • High Fidelity/ Final Designs

    I also designed the dashboard which enables the internal team to:
    Manage Nannies
    • Review new nanny applications
    • Track training completion
    • Approve or decline onboarding

    Manage Parents/Clients
    • View parent registrations
    • Track bookings
    • Assist with disputes or inquiries
    • Monitor feedback and satisfaction

  • Project Outcome

    • Reduced parent inquiry time from an average of 3 days to under 24 hours • Streamlined nanny vetting workflow, cutting administrative steps by 40% • Zero duplicate records in the admin dashboard after implementing structured input fields and validation rules • Positioned ChildcarePro as a premium, safety-focused childcare platform in early communications • Received strong social traction with 300+ combined interactions on launch-related posts
