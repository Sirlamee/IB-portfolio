  • Project Overview

    Minzubox is an AI-powered platform that allows anyone to turn an idea into a fully functional mini-app in minutes without writing a single line of code. Users simply describe what they want, and Minzubox automatically generates the front-end, back-end, and logic, creating a personalized tool that fits seamlessly into their workflow

  • Project Duration

    August 2025 - November 2025

  • My Role

    Lead UI/UX Designer, Motion Designer

  • Tools used

    Figma, Adobe Illustrator, Blender, Adobe after effect

  • Problem statement

    People juggle multiple tools for simple daily tasks. This result in:
    • Subscription overload
    • Context switching
    • Fragmented workflows

    Users can’t customize most software to fit how they work. Most users can’t control:
    • Custom workflows
    • Custom features
    • Custom automations

  • Objective / goal

    Makes AI app creation feel natural and conversational
    • Reduces friction for non-technical users
    • Highlights simplicity, focus, and speed
    • Creates a sense of ownership over users’ personalized app space
    • Communicates trust and capability without overwhelming the user

  • Design Challenges

    Creating a Personal App Space That Feels Organized: With users generating multiple mini-apps, the challenge was designing a clean, structured, and scalable environment that doesn’t become chaotic over time.

  • Design Goals

    Make app creation accessible to everyone: Reduce the complexity of app development and allow users to build functional tools using plain language.

  • Design Process

    Research:
    The research phase focused on understanding users’ pain points with existing tools, expectations from AI platforms, and the psychological barriers non-technical people face when building software.
    Key Research Activities
    • Competitive Analysis: Studied no-code and AI-building tools to understand usability gaps, onboarding complexity, and common user frustrations.
    • User Interviews: Spoke with freelancers, creators, small business owners, and productivity focused users to learn how they currently manage workflows and where their tools fail them

  • Design Process

    Ideation Goals:
    • Make app creation feel like a conversation, not a technical task.
    • Remove unnecessary steps and let the AI handle complexity.
    • Provide clear guidance so users always know “what happens next.”
    • Keep the interface minimal while still offering optional customization.

  • Design Process

    Wireframe Deliverable:
    • Onboarding screens explaining how Minzubox works in simple terms.
    • Prompt input screens optimized for natural text input and guidance.
    • AI generation feedback screens with progress indicators and explanations.
    • App customization layouts with simple toggles and visual controls.
    • Personal app space structure for organizing all generated mini-apps.

    The wireframes acted as the foundation for the visual design stage, ensuring every element served clarity, simplicity, and user empowerment.

  • High Fidelity/ Final Designs

    Font Names:

    For Minzubox font, I selected Made by Tommy Soft and Urbanist to create a visual identity that feels modern, intelligent, and effortlessly simple

  • High Fidelity/ Final Designs

    Brand colours:

    For Minzubox colour, I selected cornflower Blue (#6e81e2) as the brand primary colour and a combination of (#000000 and #364dbe) as the background colour to ensure aesthetics and sophistication.

  • High Fidelity/ Final Designs

    A Multi-Disciplinary Approach to Design

    Being able to switch between UI/UX, 3D design, and motion graphics became a core advantage in this project. Different design disciplines came together to create a unified result that was:
    • Visually expressive
    • Technically seamless
    • Emotionally engaging
    • Functionally clear

    In modern product design, the ability to work across multiple software and disciplines is becoming essential and this project is a perfect example of how blending UI/UX, Blender 3D, and motion design can elevate a digital product far beyond standard interfaces.

  • Project Outcome

    • 32 Early adopters signed up for access within the first week of announcement.
    • 70% of testers successfully created a working mini app without onboarding assistance.
    • High usability score from early test users evaluating clarity and simplicity.
