  • Project Overview

    PlansDeck is a comprehensive business and financial planning software designed to support small and medium-sized businesses, founders, and early stage entrepreneurs. The platform provides a full suite of smart tools from creating investor ready business plans to generating financial forecasts and pitch decks.

  • Project Duration

    August 2024 - May 2025

  • My Role

    UI/UX Designer

  • Product scope

    Full product design. over 100+ screens, user flows, interaction models, design system, dashboard UX, and component architecture.

  • Tools used

    Figma, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe After Effects

  • Problem statement

    Most SMEs struggle with:
    • Poorly structured business plans
    • Limited financial knowledge
    • Difficulty forecasting cashflow and revenue
    • No experience preparing investor materials
    • Fragmented tools spread across multiple platforms

  • Objective / goal

    Create an interface that simplifies heavy financial processes Offer guided tools for business plans, pitch decks, and forecasts Reduce complexity with smart UX patterns & modular screens Build a scalable system capable of supporting 100+ screens Ensure clarity for users with varying levels of financial literacy Provide a high-trust visual experience suitable for investor-facing products

  • Research Insight

    • Users fear finance-heavy tools: SME owners worry about accuracy, financial jargon, and making the “wrong inputs.”
    • Guidance increases confidence: Users prefer step-by-step flows instead of blank templates.
    • Visual clarity boosts comprehension: Charts, summaries, and structured sections are essential in making financial data digestible.
    • Collaboration matters: Many businesses involve teams. PlansDeck needed features that allow multiple contributors to work seamlessly.
    • Export quality is crucial: Pitch decks and business plans must look clean, professional, and investor-ready.
    These insights shaped the UX strategy from structure to visual hierarchy.

  • UX Approach

    Information Architecture/User flow:
    With over 100 screens to be designed, the IA had to be,
    • Clean
    • Scalable
    • Logical
    • Organized by workflows

  • Design Process

    Wireframe Deliverable:
    To avoid the typical “linear wireframe approach,” I designed using a modular workflow architecture.
    My wireframing strategy included:
    • Mapping interconnected flows (instead of isolated screens)
    • Designing reusable structural templates for consistency
    • Creating data-driven layouts for financial charts & summaries
    • Building decision tree flows for guided business plan creation
    • Using adaptive panels to support different business types

    This allowed me to rapidly scale the product from 20 to 100+ screens without breaking visual consistency.

  • Design System

    To support over 100 screens, I built a full design system, including:
    • 40+ reusable components
    • Extensive grid + spacing logic
    • Color tokens & semantic layers
    • Typography scale
    • Interaction states (hover, pressed, loading)

  • High Fidelity/ Final Designs

    The high-fidelity direction was built to emphasize clarity, trust, and intelligence.
    Design Priorities
    • Minimal but sophisticated color palette
    • Clear typography hierarchy for readability
    • Strong spacing system for calming visual flow
    • Clean card-based layout for financial and business data
    • Smart section dividers for long-form business plans
    • Iconography built for speed and recognizability

  • Final Screens (Key deliverable)

    • Business Plan Builder
    A guided, section-based editor with prompts, examples, and real-time formatting.
    • Pitch Deck Generator
    Investor-ready slides with customizable layouts and auto-content suggestions.
    • Financial Forecast Engine
    Advanced modeling tools with charts, projections, and automated summaries.
    • Financial Review Dashboard
    Visual insights showing business performance at a glance.
    • Milestone & Roadmap Manager
    A timeline-style planning tool for tracking growth and execution.
    • Collaboration & Export Tools
    Clean export feature for PDF, slides, and documents.
    • Settings, Team Management, and Profile
    All grouped under a simplified global navigation system.

  • Success Metrics

    • 100+ screens delivered with full consistency
    • 40% faster workflow during development due to the scalable design system
    • 70% reduction in user confusion during usability testing
    Plansdeck is currently in their beta testing and will be launching fully soon
