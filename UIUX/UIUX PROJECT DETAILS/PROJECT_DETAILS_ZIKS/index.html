  • Project Overview

    Ziks is a merchant facing dashboard designed for e-commerce businesses to manage a powerful AI chatbot that handles customer conversations across multiple social platforms including Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Shopify Chat, and website chat widgets.

  • Project Duration

    October 2025 - January 2026

  • My Role

    Lead UI/UX Designer

  • Tools used

    Figma, Adobe Illustrator.

  • Problem statement

    1. No 24/7 Availability:
    Customers message at all hours, and delayed replies often lead to abandoned carts or lost sales.
    2. Scattered Conversations:
    Businesses manage messages across WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and website chats — creating chaos, slow response times, and missed orders.
    3. Manual Work is Overwhelming:
    Responding to FAQs, product inquiries, delivery details, and order tracking consumes time that merchants should spend on growth.

  • Objective / goal

    • Accesses store inventory through API connections and provides accurate availability
    • Closes sales automatically, encouraging customers to check out or pay immediately
    • Hands off conversations to a human agent when nuance or escalation is needed
    • Learns from past interactions, improving tone, accuracy, and recommendations over time

  • Design Challenges

    Unifying Multiple Chat Platforms Into One Experience: Each platform (WhatsApp, IG, Facebook, Shopify, Web Chat) has different message structures, APIs, rate limits, and interaction styles. Creating a clean, consistent conversation interface required abstracting all channels into a unified format without losing platform-specific context.

  • Design Goals

    • Provide a single UI where merchants can manage all incoming messages from every platform.
    • Increase merchant response speed to 100% Ensure no message is missed, even outside working hours.
    • Turn conversations into revenue, enable the AI to close sales automatically, follow up on leads, and provide checkout links.
    • Build trust between merchants and the AI. Allow users to monitor, edit, and improve the AI’s behavior.

  • Design Process

    Research:
    Key Research Activities
    • Interviews with e-commerce sellers across WhatsApp, IG, and Shopify
    • Analysis of customer support patterns common in Nigerian markets
    • Competitor review of chat automation tools, CRMs, and inbox unification platforms
    • Observation sessions to see how merchants handle peak-hour conversations

    Key Insights
    • Most merchants struggle during high-traffic periods (evenings + weekends).
    • Important messages often get lost in Instagram or WhatsApp DMs.
    • Merchants prefer simple, quick actions over complex settings.
    • Trust is essential — merchants want visibility into everything the AI says.

  • Design Process

    The ideation phase explored ways to simplify complex multi-channel messaging and AI automation into a smooth, merchant-friendly experience.

    Ideation Goals:
    • Design intuitive tools for editing AI behavior.
    • Make analytics understandable to non-technical users.
    • Integrate store inventory seamlessly into conversations.
    • Provide quick actions for human takeover when needed.

    Concept Development
    • Unified Chat Interface that merges all messages into one format.
    • AI Activity Timeline showing what the AI responded and why.
    • Inventory-Synced Replies giving real-time product availability.
    • Smart Alerts when the AI is unsure or needs human assistance.

  • UX approach

    Competitive Analysis

    Before moving deeper into the design process, I conducted a competitive analysis to understand how existing conversational commerce and support platforms serve merchants today. I evaluated tools across four categories: chat automation, multi-platform inboxes, AI assistants, and e-commerce support tools.

  • UX approach

    User Journey Map

    To understand how merchants interact with Ziks from first touch to daily usage, I created a user journey map outlining their motivations, actions, pain points, and emotional state at each stage.

  • UX approach

    Site Map

    After mapping the end-to-end user journey, I created a comprehensive sitemap to define the structural hierarchy of the Ziks dashboard. With multiple features—messaging, AI training, analytics, integrations, automation, and inventory—clarity in navigation was essential.

  • UX approach

    I translated core user scenarios into detailed user flows to ensure frictionless navigation. These covered critical actions such as:
    • Connecting social platforms
    • Responding to a customer message
    • Letting the AI auto-respond
    • Escalating to a human
    • Viewing analytics
    • Updating inventory
    • Adding knowledge for AI training

  • Design Process

    Wireframe Properties

    After laying out all the necessary UX foundation i decided to dive into drawing out the wireframes
    • Clear separation between AI replies and human replies
    • Easy navigation between platforms, customers, and orders
    • Scannable chat layout optimized for speed
    • Inventory panel accessible during conversations
    • Analytics dashboard designed for business clarity

  • Design Process

    Before moving into high fidelity design, I established a robust design system to ensure visual consistency, scalability, and efficiency across the entire Ziks dashboard. Because the platform involves multiple complex interfaces, chat management, analytics, AI training, inventory, and automation. A well structured system was essential.

    To support development scalability and maintain consistency across dozens of screens, I built a library of reusable components, including:
    • Buttons, toggles, and dropdowns
    • Chat bubbles with platform-specific variations
    • AI status indicators and message tags
    • Table systems for orders, inventory, and customer data
    • Cards for analytics, insights, and quick actions
    • Form elements used in AI knowledge base and settings

  • High Fidelity/ Final Designs

    With the wireframes validated and design system ready, I transitioned into high-fidelity design bringing Ziks to life with visual clarity, strong hierarchy, and a consistent design language built from the system I established earlier.
    The challenge was to create an interface that feels powerful yet extremely simple, considering the diverse skill level of Nigerian e-commerce merchants. Every pixel was shaped around speed, readability, and confidence.

  • Final Experience

    Working on Ziks was a deeply rewarding experience that pushed me to grow as a product designer in several meaningful ways. Because the platform spans messaging, AI automation, analytics, inventory, and multi-platform integrations, the project required a level of UX depth and structural thinking far beyond a standard dashboard design.

    With the full design system, user flows, and high-fidelity screens completed, Ziks has moved into development. I continue to support the build phase through design QA and iteration.
